Crimson Fist – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Data we collect

Crimson Fist does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (unlocked levels and star ratings) is saved locally on your device only and is never sent to us.

In-app purchase

The optional "Full Game" purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details or personal information.

Children

The game collects no information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/svashchenko8877/crimsonfist/issues.
